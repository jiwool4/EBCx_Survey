2026 BCxA Existing Building Commissioning (EBCx) Market Survey

Fields marked * are required.

The purpose of this market survey is to determine how the building commissioning industry approaches EBCx projects. We recognize that not all EBCx projects are the same and that the work scope will vary by Cx provider and project. We hope to have a representative from each branch office complete this survey and identify the tasks you address as part of an average EBCx project.

For example, if a specific task requires 5% of a typical project budget but is only included in the work scope for 20% of your office's projects, then that task would represent 1% of the budget for an average EBCx project. While the percentage of total budget dedicated to each task is important, the most valuable distinctions we expect to make are identifying tasks that Cx providers do not include in their EBCx work — those requiring 0% of their project budgets.

Survey objectives
  • What tasks are part of a typical industry EBCx project?
  • How much of the total budget is dedicated to each task?
  • Which EBCx tasks are providers not supporting?
  • What additional costs would owners bear for unsupported tasks?
  • What systems are being commissioned in EBCx investigations?
  • What issues are being identified in those investigations?
How to complete this survey
  • Move through Tabs 2 → 3 → 4 in order.
  • Fill in only the editable (white) fields.
  • Use the live totals bars to balance percentages to 100%.
  • Rather than overthink responses, enter values proportional to time spent — you can refine as you go.
  • Acronyms are defined in Tab 6.
  • Submit using the button at the bottom of Tab 4.
